How the Embroidery Pricing Calculator Works

Pricing custom embroidery by guesswork either leaves money on the table when a dense logo takes twenty minutes to sew, or scares away repeat team orders when a simple left-chest design is overpriced. This calculator reads your Tajima (.dst) or Brother (.pes) file directly in your browser, counts the exact normal stitches and color changes, estimates machine run time, and builds a quantity break table and copyable quote in one step.

Step-by-Step Workflow

  1. Drop your design file or use manual mode: Drag a .dst or .pes file into the box above. A local browser Web Worker reads every stitch record to extract normal stitches, jumps, trims, color changes, and width × height in inches and millimeters. Don't have the file yet? Click Enter stitch count instead and type your stitch count and colors.
  2. Review or override any design number: Every extracted value—stitch count, thread colors, color changes, dimensions, and estimated minutes per piece—can be edited directly to test "what-if" scenarios.
  3. Set the order and garment details: Enter the piece quantity, choose whether the customer supplies the blank garment or you supply it with a markup percentage, and toggle any one-time digitizing or setup fee.
  4. Customize your shop rates: Open Shop Rate Settings & Machine Speed to enter your own rate per 1,000 stitches, per-piece handling fee, minimum decoration charge, extra-color threshold, quantity break discounts, and machine speed. Your settings are saved in your browser's local storage for your next visit.
  5. Send or print the quote: Copy the plain-text quote into an email or message, or click Print / Save as PDF for a single-page letter quote.

Worked Example: 24 Custom Caps (8,000 Stitches, 3 Colors)

Using the calculator's starting values ($1.50 per 1,000 stitches, $1.00 per-piece fee, $5.00 minimum, 6 colors included, and a 10% quantity break for 24–47 pieces):

  • Base decoration price: (8,000 ÷ 1,000) × $1.50 + $1.00 = $13.00
  • Discounted decoration at 24 pieces (10% off): $13.00 × (1 − 0.10) = $11.70 per piece (above the $5.00 minimum)
  • Order total (customer-supplied caps, $0 setup): $11.70 × 24 = $280.80
  • Machine time per piece: 8,000 ÷ 650 spm + (2 color changes × 0.5 min) + 1.0 min hooping = 14.31 minutes
  • Effective hourly rate: ($11.70 ÷ 14.31 min) × 60 = $49.06/hr

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my DST or PES design file get uploaded to a server?

No. Your design file never leaves your computer. The file is parsed inside a local browser Web Worker using JavaScript, and no network request ever transmits your design data.

Are jump stitches and trims included in the priced stitch count?

By default, only normal sewing stitches are used in the stitch-rate formula, while jumps and trims are displayed separately so you can see how clean the file is. You can override the priced stitch count at any time.

Why does the calculator round the per-piece price before multiplying by quantity?

Rounding the unit price to the nearest cent (half up) before multiplying by the order quantity guarantees that the line item on your customer's quote multiplies out cleanly by hand.
